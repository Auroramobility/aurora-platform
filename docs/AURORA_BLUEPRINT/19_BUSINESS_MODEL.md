# Aurora Business Model

## Executive Summary

Aurora is an electric vehicle ownership platform that combines technology, education, transparent ownership, and strategic partnerships to make premium electric vehicles more accessible.

Aurora is not simply a vehicle marketplace.

Aurora provides an ecosystem that helps customers discover, compare, finance, own, and manage electric vehicles through a transparent and technology-driven experience.

Our business model is built around long-term customer relationships rather than one-time vehicle transactions.

---

# Business Philosophy

Aurora believes sustainable businesses create value for every participant.

Customers should benefit.

Dealers should benefit.

Partners should benefit.

Investors should benefit.

Manufacturers should benefit.

Aurora succeeds when everyone succeeds.

---

# The Problem

Millions of people want electric vehicles but face barriers such as:

- High purchase prices
- Traditional financing complexity
- High interest rates
- Limited understanding of EV ownership
- Lack of transparent information
- Limited access in emerging markets

Aurora exists to remove these barriers.

---

# Aurora's Solution

Aurora combines multiple services into one platform.

These include:

- EV Marketplace

Each service strengthens the others.
