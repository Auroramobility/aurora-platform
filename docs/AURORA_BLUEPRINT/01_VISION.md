# Aurora Vision

## Why Aurora Exists

The world is transitioning toward electric mobility.

Unfortunately, buying an electric vehicle is still confusing for many people.

Questions about charging, battery life, maintenance, range, financing, and ownership often discourage potential buyers.

Aurora exists to remove those barriers.

Aurora is designed to become the trusted guide for anyone considering electric transportation.

---

# What Aurora Is

Aurora is an intelligent electric mobility platform.

Aurora combines:

- Marketplace
- Vehicle Comparison
- Education
- AI Guidance
- Financing
- Ownership Tools

into one seamless experience.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Aurora should become the first destination people visit before purchasing an electric vehicle.

Whether someone wants to:

- Learn
- Compare
- Calculate ownership costs
- Browse inventory
- Finance a vehicle
- Reserve a vehicle
- Speak with an EV expert

Aurora should provide the answer.
