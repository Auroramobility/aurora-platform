# Aurora Mobility — Product Blueprint

Version: 1.0
Status: Living Document

---

# Mission

Aurora Mobility exists to make electric vehicle ownership accessible, affordable, and transparent for everyday people through flexible financing and a trusted ownership journey.

Aurora is not simply an online dealership.

Aurora is an EV ownership platform that helps people move from wanting an electric vehicle to confidently owning one.

---

# Vision

To become the most trusted platform for electric vehicle ownership by combining technology, financing, education, and community into one seamless experience.

---

# Core Values

- Accessibility
- Transparency
- Sustainability
- Trust
- Community
- Innovation

---

# The Problem

Millions of people want to own electric vehicles.

However, many face barriers such as:

- High upfront costs
- Limited financing options
- Lack of trust
- Limited understanding of EV ownership
- Complicated application processes

Aurora removes those barriers.

---

# Our Solution

Aurora provides a guided ownership experience.

Users can:

- Learn
- Browse
- Compare
- Estimate affordability
- Apply
- Track their application
- Own their vehicle

Everything happens inside one platform.
