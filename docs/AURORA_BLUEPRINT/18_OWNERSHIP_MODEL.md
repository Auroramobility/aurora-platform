# 18 Ownership Model

Define Aurora's ownership model, including vehicle lifecycle support, ownership tools, maintenance tracking, and retention strategies.# Aurora Ownership Model

## Overview

Aurora exists to redefine how people own premium electric vehicles.

Traditional vehicle financing often creates barriers through high interest rates, hidden fees, complex contracts, and limited accessibility.

Aurora introduces a transparent, equity-first ownership model designed to make premium electric vehicles more attainable while ensuring customers understand exactly how their payments contribute toward ownership.

Aurora is built on the belief that vehicle ownership should be fair, transparent, and understandable.

---

# Vision

To make premium electric vehicle ownership accessible to everyone by removing financial barriers and redefining how people own electric vehicles through transparent, equity-first financing.

---

# Mission

Empower customers through an ownership platform where every payment contributes toward legally recognized vehicle ownership while providing transparency throughout the entire ownership journey.

---

# Core Principles

## Transparency

Customers should always know:

- Vehicle price
- Payment schedule
- Ownership progress
- Remaining balance
- Contract status
- Vehicle history

Nothing should be hidden.

---

## Fair Ownership

Every payment should create measurable progress toward ownership.

Aurora should always present ownership progress clearly.

---

## Accessibility

Aurora is designed for customers who may not have access to traditional financing.

The platform should simplify ownership rather than complicate it.

---

## Trust

Trust is Aurora's most valuable asset.

Every recommendation, calculation, document, and payment should reinforce customer confidence.

---

## Step 1

↓

## Step 2

- Vehicle specifications

- Total ownership cost

## Step 3

↓

## Step 4

Aurora verifies eligibility.

↓

## Step 5

Ownership agreement is created.

↓

## Step 6

Customer begins making scheduled payments.

↓

## Step 7

Each successful payment updates:

- Ownership percentage
- Remaining balance
- Payment history

↓

Ownership obligations are fulfilled according to the agreement.

↓

## Step 9
